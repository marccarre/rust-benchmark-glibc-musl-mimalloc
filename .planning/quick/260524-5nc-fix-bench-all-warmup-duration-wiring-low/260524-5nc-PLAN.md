<objective>
Fix the `bench-all` warmup/duration wiring so the canonical local statistical-quality run actually uses 5s warmup + 60s measure (it was hardcoded to 1s/5s smoke shape), and lower the aggregator's suspect-samples threshold from `< 10_000` to `< 1_000` so healthy 60s runs of slow scenarios (e.g. cpu-bound ~10 800 samples, multithread under tick-latency pressure) stop being incorrectly flagged.

Purpose: Restore CLAUDE.md's documented contract — `just bench-all` should be the "canonical local statistical-quality run" (warmup 5s, duration 60s) while `just bench-all-smoke` remains the "CI-shape recipe" (warmup 1s, duration 5s). Eliminate the noisy `⚠ suspect: low samples` flags that currently decorate almost every cell of REPORT.md when running the full 60s shape.

Output: Updated CLI surface, justfile recipes, aggregator threshold, fixture, and CLAUDE.md convention bullet — all in one cohesive change set.
</objective>
