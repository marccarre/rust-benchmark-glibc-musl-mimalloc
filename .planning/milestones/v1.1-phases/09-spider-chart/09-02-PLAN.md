<objective>
Land the data-only foundations for POLAR-05 (Pareto-front overlay column on the `## Top 10 cells` summary table + per-cell card `★` glyph). Two surgical edits, both confined to `score.rs` and `recommend.rs` — no template, no markdown, no html.rs work in this plan (those land in Plan 03 once both Wave-1 plans are complete).

Purpose: The Pareto front of (composite_score ↑, image_size_mb ↓) is a cross-cutting decoration applied to top-N cells in TWO consumer surfaces (summary table + per-cell template). Computing the front in `score.rs` (sibling to `top_n`) keeps the math unit-testable in isolation; populating `is_pareto` inside `top_n_cells` keeps the field on the `CellRecommendation` struct so both consumers (markdown summary table + html per-cell template) see the same flag without re-running the Pareto sweep.

Output: `score::pareto_front` (new pub fn alongside `top_n`); `CellRecommendation::is_pareto` (new pub bool field); `top_n_cells` widened signature accepting `image_sizes: &BTreeMap<String, f64>` and populating `is_pareto` per cell.
