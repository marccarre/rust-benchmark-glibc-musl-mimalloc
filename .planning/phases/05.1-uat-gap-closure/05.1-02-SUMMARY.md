## IN-03 Deferred-Finding Link

Per the plan's `<output>` block: this Gap 2 fix is the production-driven realization of the IN-03 review note ("the mv stanza is too clever") from `05-REVIEW.md`. The reviewer worried the mv pattern would over-match (future non-seed JSONs leaking into `meta/`); the production failure was the inverse — under-matching to zero files due to the path-level mismatch with upload-artifact@v4's preserved-subdirectory contract.

**The broader IN-03 hardening remains DEFERRED and OUT OF SCOPE for Plan 05.1-02.** A future hardening plan (likely scoped to v1.1 or a dedicated CI-hardening phase) can revisit:
- Replacing the mv-glob-then-aggregate pattern with extglob (`shopt -s extglob; mv artifacts/results/!(*.tmp)*.json results/`)
- Migrating per-cell artifacts to use `name:` patterns that already match the aggregator's expected directory layout (eliminating the Reorganize step entirely)
- Adding a JSON-schema validation step between the Reorganize step and the aggregator invocation (catches malformed cells early)

The minimal fix in Plan 05.1-02 closes the actual production blocker; the broader hardening is a quality-of-life improvement and not on any milestone's critical path.
